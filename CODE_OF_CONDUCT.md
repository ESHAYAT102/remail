# Code of conduct

This project is a shared workplace. Be kind, be direct, and leave the work better than you found it.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect, including maintainers, contributors, and people filing issues.
- Assume good intent, then ask a precise question.
- Critique ideas and diffs, not people.
- Do not gatekeep: newcomers are welcome if they follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Not welcome

Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind. Publishing private information. Spam, sealioning, or using this project to advertise unrelated work. Anything illegal.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official Remail space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, hide, or ban. Report a problem to the maintainers listed on the repository. Reports are handled privately.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), kept short on purpose.
